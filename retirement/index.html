What this means

Educational illustration only. Not investment advice or a guarantee. Monte Carlo uses 3,000 independently simulated annual portfolio returns drawn from a normal distribution with user-entered arithmetic mean and standard deviation. Withdrawals occur at each year-end, increase with inflation, and fees reduce the invested balance annually. Returns are gross of fees; withdrawals are assumed to be funded entirely from investments. No Social Security, pensions, contributions, taxes, or spending adjustments are modeled. The normal-return assumption does not capture fat tails or persistent market regimes. The model has not been independently validated for production client use.
